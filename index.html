    How to use the Rate Card Builder

    1. Customer: enter who the rate card is for and your contact details.
    2. Equipment: untick sections or individual items the customer doesn't need. You can also hover a line in the preview and press ×. To change a price, type in its box. Changed prices turn yellow, and ↺ puts the list price back. Use + Add item for equipment that isn't on the list.
    3. Pricing: choose which rate columns to show, or apply a percentage to many rates at once.
    4. Save keeps the rate card on this computer so you can reopen it later. Save a copy to a file (in the ••• menu) lets you keep it in OneDrive or send it to a colleague.
    5. Publish PDF creates the PDF to send to your customer.

    Update rates (admin)

    1. Download the rates spreadsheet

    Opens in Excel. Each row is one line of the rate card. Change prices and descriptions, or add, delete and reorder rows. Keep the ID column as it is, and leave it blank for new rows. Type is one of SECTION, SUB (grey bar), GROUP (bold label), ITEM, NOTE (red text), TEXT (black text) or IMAGE. Leave all three rate cells blank to show “PLEASE INQUIRE”.

    2. Upload your edited spreadsheet

    In Excel, use File › Save As › CSV UTF-8 (Comma delimited). Uploading updates this window so you can check the preview first.
