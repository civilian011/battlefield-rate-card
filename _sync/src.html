Rate columns on the rate card
Adjust rates by percentage

Raise or discount the list price separately for each rate column. Use a negative number for a discount, e.g. -10. Enter 0 to keep the list price. Leave a box empty to use the broader adjustment, or the list price if there isn't one. Rates are rounded to the dollar.

If adjustments overlap, the most specific one is used for each rate column: a product group's adjustment wins over its category's, and a category's wins over “All equipment”. Prices typed on the Equipment tab win over every adjustment. On the Equipment tab, adjusted rates are blue and typed prices are yellow.

Publish PDF

  1. Click Continue. Your browser's print window opens.
  2. Set Destination (or Printer) to Save as PDF (in Edge or Chrome) or Microsoft Print to PDF.
  3. Click Save, choose where to save it, and send the PDF to your customer.

Your rate card is saved automatically when you publish.

    Review rate card

    How to use the Rate Card Builder

    1. Customer: enter who the rate card is for and your contact details.
    2. Equipment: untick categories or individual items the customer doesn't need. You can also hover a line in the preview and press ×. To put the most important categories first, drag a category by its handle (⋮⋮) or use its ▲ ▼ arrows. To change a price, type in its box. Typed prices turn yellow, and ↺ puts the price back. Use + Add item for equipment that isn't on the list.
    3. Pricing: choose which rate columns to show, and raise or discount Daily, Weekly and 4 Weeks rates by different percentages for all equipment, a category or a product group.
    4. Save keeps the rate card on this computer so you can reopen it later. Save a copy to a file (in the ••• menu) lets you keep it in OneDrive or send it to a colleague.
    5. Publish PDF creates the PDF to send to your customer.
    6. Export to CSV for MOAB downloads the customer's special pricing for upload into MOAB: every item whose price differs from the list price, plus your custom items. Enter the Customer number on the Customer tab first, and a Klass code for each custom item.

    Update rates (admin)

    1. Download the rates spreadsheet

    Opens in Excel. Each row is one line of the rate card. Change prices and descriptions, or add, delete and reorder rows. Keep the ID column as it is, and leave it blank for new rows. Klass holds the ERP Klass code(s) of an item, separated by commas. Type is one of SECTION, SUB (grey bar), GROUP (bold label), ITEM, NOTE (red text), TEXT (black text) or IMAGE. Leave all three rate cells blank to show “PLEASE INQUIRE”.

    2. Upload your edited spreadsheet

    In Excel, use File › Save As › CSV UTF-8 (Comma delimited). Uploading updates this window so you can check the preview first.
